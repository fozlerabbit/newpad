Privacy at Pad Koi

You should be able to find a pad without having to explain who you are.

No account required

You can search, view the map and get directions without signing up or logging in.

Location used only to find nearby places

If you allow location access, it is used in your browser to centre the map and calculate distances — it is not sent to or stored on a Pad Koi server.

No location history

Pad Koi does not keep a record of where you have searched or which stores you have viewed.

Minimal data collection

We do not ask for your gender, name or personal profile to use the core search features. Store submissions only collect what's needed to list a location.

No unnecessary analytics or tracking

Pad Koi avoids invasive tracking. Any future usage metrics will be aggregated and anonymised.

Community submissions

If you submit a store through the Add a Store form, only the information you choose to provide (store name, address, contact details, product information) is collected, and it is reviewed before being shown as verified.

Questions

If you have a question about how Pad Koi handles information, contact hello@scriptysphere.example.
