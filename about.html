About Pad Koi

Pad Koi is a project of ScriptySphere. It helps people find nearby stores, pharmacies, emergency points and community resources for menstrual products — quickly, privately and without stigma.

The problem

Menstrual health is not only about whether products exist in a country or community — it is also about whether a person, in the moment they need it, can find where a product actually is. Pad Koi addresses this specific gap: discoverability.

How it works

Pad Koi combines a location-based directory, an interactive map and community-supported availability information so that a person can search for their area, see nearby stores and pharmacies, and get directions — all without creating an account.

Who it's for

Pad Koi is built for anyone who may need to find a menstrual product: women and girls, students, parents, teachers, friends and caregivers. Menstrual health is a community responsibility, not a subject only some people should understand.

Our approach

  • Privacy by design — no account, no unnecessary data collection
  • Transparent availability — clear verified, community-reported and unknown states
  • Community-powered mapping, with responsible verification
  • Built to scale to schools, universities, NGOs and pharmacies

ScriptySphere

Pad Koi is developed and maintained by ScriptySphere, a programming and social-impact initiative. Pad Koi is one of several projects ScriptySphere builds at the intersection of technology and community wellbeing.
